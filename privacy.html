Privacy Policy

Placeholder content for this demo site. The final policy is supplied by Highflow Performance Ltd before launch.

1. What we collect

When you contact the workshop we collect the details you provide: your name, phone number, email address and information about your vehicle and the work you want done.

2. How we use it

Your details are used to respond to enquiries, prepare quotes, book work and keep records of work carried out on your vehicle. We do not sell or share your information with third parties for marketing.

3. Storage

Records are held securely and kept only as long as needed for workshop records, warranty and legal purposes.

4. Your rights

Under the Privacy Act 2020 you can ask what personal information we hold about you, and ask for it to be corrected or deleted. Ring 021 136 7162 to make a request.

5. Website

This website does not use contact forms and does not set marketing cookies. The embedded Google Map is served by Google and subject to Google's own privacy policy.
