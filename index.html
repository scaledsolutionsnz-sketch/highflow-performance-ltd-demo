A fabrication workshop, not a parts fitter

Highflow Performance is Levi's workshop at Omanawa, in the hills behind Tauranga. The work is proper fabrication: manifolds built from scratch, full stainless systems, engine and gearbox mounts machined to fit, conversions wired and tuned on Link ECUs.

Recent builds include a JZX100 Chaser running an RB25 NEO with a big-box conversion, custom mounts and a full 3 inch stainless exhaust, all made in-house and built to be certed and reliable.

  • One fabricator on your car, start to finish
  • Built with cert in mind from the first tack weld
  • A straight quote before any work starts
