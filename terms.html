Quotes are provided in writing before work begins and remain valid for 30 days. Custom fabrication work is quoted per job. If a job uncovers additional work once a vehicle is stripped, you will be contacted for approval before that work proceeds.

All fabrication and mechanical work is carried out to a professional standard. Any workmanship concern raised within a reasonable period will be inspected and, where the workshop is at fault, remedied at no cost.

Vehicles are left at the workshop at the owner's risk. Reasonable care is taken while vehicles are on site, and owners are asked to remove valuables before drop-off.

4. Payment

Payment is due on completion of work unless another arrangement has been agreed in writing. Vehicles and fabricated parts remain the property of Highflow Performance Ltd until paid for in full.

Where work requires LVV certification, certification is carried out by an independent certifier and their fees are separate from workshop charges unless included in the quote.
